Bespoke Timber Bifold Doors

Bifold doors transform the relationship between inside and outside. They fold open to create a wide, uninterrupted opening — connecting your kitchen, living room, or extension to a garden, terrace, or patio. At Prime Sash Windows, we manufacture bespoke timber bifold doors from 2 to 8 panels, built to your exact opening dimensions.

Unlike aluminium or uPVC alternatives, our timber bifold doors offer warmth, character, and authenticity. Engineered hardwood provides dimensional stability — no warping, no bowing — while delivering thermal performance that exceeds Building Regulations requirements.

Specification

  • Panels — 2 to 8 panels. Maximum panel width 950mm.
  • Timber — engineered Accoya or hardwood. 50-year rot guarantee (Accoya).
  • Frame — 50mm face, 95mm depth. Leaf section 94mm × 65mm.
  • Glazing — double glazed 4/16/4 (U-value 1.4) or triple glazed 4/12/4/12/4 (U-value 0.8).
  • Security — PAS24 multi-point locking. Secured by Design option available.
  • Threshold — Standard hardwood, aluminium low-profile, or flush aluminium.
  • Colour — any RAL colour, full Farrow & Ball range. Dual colour (different interior/exterior) available.
  • FENSA registered — Building Regulations self-certification on completion.

Timber vs Aluminium Bifold Doors

Aluminium bifolds dominate the mass market, but timber offers clear advantages for discerning homeowners. Timber is a natural insulator — it conducts heat 1,500 times less than aluminium, meaning fewer cold spots and lower energy bills. Timber can be repaired and refinished indefinitely, while aluminium cannot. And timber bifolds suit period properties, Conservation Areas, and Listed buildings where aluminium may not be acceptable.

Our engineered timber construction eliminates the warping risk associated with solid timber. Accoya — acetylated radiata pine — is dimensionally stable, rot-proof, and comes with a 50-year structural guarantee.

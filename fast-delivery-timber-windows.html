🇬🇧 Made in Britain ⚡ 4–5 Week Delivery

4–5 Weeks. Not 12.

Most timber window companies quote 8–12 weeks for delivery. Some stretch to 16 weeks. The reason is simple: they import windows from overseas factories. Your order joins a shipping container, crosses an ocean, clears customs, and eventually arrives at a warehouse. If anything is wrong — a colour mismatch, a measurement error, a damaged frame — you wait another 8–12 weeks for a replacement.

We manufacture every window in our workshop near London. Raw timber arrives, and finished windows leave — typically within 4–5 weeks of order confirmation. No shipping containers. No customs delays. No overseas middlemen. If a problem arises, we fix it in days, not months.

Why We Are Faster

UK Workshop, Not a Foreign Factory

Our workshop is near London. We control every stage of production: timber preparation, machining, assembly, glazing, painting, and quality inspection. There is no supply chain stretching across continents. When your order is confirmed, it goes straight into our production schedule.

Direct to You

We sell direct — no distributors, no showroom chains, no regional agents. Your order goes from our workshop to your property. Fewer steps means fewer delays.

Stock Timber Ready to Go

We maintain stock of the most popular timber sections (engineered Accoya and premium hardwood) in standard profiles. This eliminates the wait for raw material that delays many manufacturers. When your order arrives, the timber is already in our workshop.

For standard configurations (sash windows, casement windows, French doors in standard sizes and colours), 4–5 weeks from order to delivery is our standard lead time. Bespoke designs — arched windows, unusual shapes, or complex configurations — may require additional time, which we confirm at the point of order.

When Fast Delivery Matters Most

Speed is not a luxury — in many situations, it is essential. Builders working to completion deadlines cannot afford a 12-week wait for windows. Homeowners preparing a property for sale need work finished quickly. Renovation projects with scaffolding up are burning money every week windows are delayed. Tenants waiting to move in, conservation officers waiting for compliance, insurance claims that need resolution — all of these need windows delivered on time.

We built our production process around speed because we understand that late windows cause real financial damage. A builder with scaffolding up for an extra 6 weeks, or a homeowner paying rent while waiting for a delayed installation, loses far more than the cost of the windows themselves.

Same-Month Delivery

For urgent orders, we offer same-month delivery on standard configurations, subject to workshop capacity. If you need windows before a specific deadline — a completion date, a tenancy start, or a building control inspection — contact us directly and we will confirm whether your timeline is achievable before you commit.

Our Expertise

Traditional Sash Windows

Every window we make follows the traditional method — built from premium hardwood, finished with multiple coats of microporous paint for lasting protection, and balanced exclusively on lead weights. We never use spring balances.

Our lead weights are 100% recycled, keeping the authentic feel and smooth operation of a proper sash window while being environmentally responsible. The result is a window that works exactly as it should — quiet, balanced, and built to last decades.

Technology

Passive Glass Technology

Traditional double glazing achieves a U-value of around 1.4 W/m²K. With Passive Glass, our windows reach U-values as low as 0.8 W/m²K — a dramatic improvement for overall building energy performance.

For architects struggling to meet SAP calculations or Part L compliance on heritage projects, this is a game-changer. No compromise between conservation requirements and energy targets.

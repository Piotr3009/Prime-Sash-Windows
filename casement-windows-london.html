Timber Casement Windows for London Properties

Casement windows are the most versatile timber window type. They suit everything from contemporary new builds to Arts and Crafts cottages, 1930s semis, and modern extensions on period properties. Unlike sash windows, casement windows hinge outward (or inward) and offer unrestricted ventilation — the entire window aperture opens.

At Prime Sash Windows, we manufacture bespoke timber casement windows in over 20 configurations. Single light, double light, triple light. With or without fanlights. Side-hung, top-hung, or fixed. Every window is made to your exact dimensions in engineered Accoya or hardwood, spray-finished in any colour.

Casement Window Styles

Flush Casement

The sash sits flush with the outer frame when closed — a clean, traditional appearance suited to conservation areas and period properties.

Stormproof Casement

The sash overlaps the frame, creating a superior weather seal. The most popular choice for new builds and extensions.

Multi-Light Casement

Two, three, or four lights in a single frame. Mix opening and fixed sections to suit ventilation and budget requirements.

Casement with Fanlight

A top-hung opening light above the main casement. Traditional appearance with excellent ventilation — the fanlight can remain open in rain.

Why Timber Casement Windows?

Thermal Performance

Timber is a natural insulator. Our casement frames achieve significantly better thermal performance than aluminium or uPVC alternatives. Combined with our double, triple, or passive glazing options (U-values down to 0.8 W/m²K), a timber casement window is the best performing window you can install.

Lifespan

A properly maintained timber casement window lasts 60 to 100 years. Our Accoya timber is guaranteed for 50 years against rot and fungal decay. Compare that with uPVC, which typically degrades within 20 to 25 years and cannot be repaired — only replaced.

Sustainability

Timber is the only renewable window frame material. Our Accoya is FSC certified and sourced from sustainably managed forests. At end of life, timber is biodegradable. uPVC is not.

Security & Compliance

  • PAS24 enhanced security — standard on every casement window, no upgrade charge
  • Part L compliant — all glazing options meet current thermal requirements
  • FENSA registered — Building Regulations self-certification and certificate
  • Espagnolette locking — multi-point locking on all opening casements
  • Fire escape compliant — egress hinges available on specified windows

St Albans's Timber Window Specialists

St Albans is one of England's most historic cities, with architecture spanning from medieval times to the present day. The city centre features stunning Georgian townhouses, elegant Victorian properties, and charming Edwardian homes, all requiring windows that respect their architectural heritage. At Prime Sash Windows, we manufacture bespoke timber sash windows that honour St Albans's heritage while delivering modern thermal and acoustic performance.

St Albans has extensive Conservation Areas protecting its historic core, including the Cathedral area, Fishpool Street, and the Victorian residential areas. Many properties also hold Listed Building status. Our experienced team understands the requirements of St Albans City and District Council and creates windows that satisfy heritage officers while delivering modern performance.

Why St Albans Homeowners Choose Us

Every window is manufactured in our workshop near London from engineered Accoya or premium hardwood. Accoya is guaranteed for 50 years against rot and fungal decay — far outlasting uPVC, which typically degrades within 20 to 25 years.

  • Slimline double glazing — 4/16/4 sealed units with warm-edge spacer bars. U-value 1.4 W/m²K. Part L compliant.
  • PAS24 security — multi-point espagnolette locking on all opening sashes.
  • Traditional lead weights — we never use spring balances. Smoother operation, longer lifespan.
  • Any colour — over 2,000 RAL colours and the full Farrow & Ball range. Dual colour available.
  • FENSA registered — Building Regulations self-certification and certificate on completion.

Every window is spray-finished with premium Sikkens coatings — a multi-coat system including primer, undercoat, and topcoat for a flawless, lasting finish.

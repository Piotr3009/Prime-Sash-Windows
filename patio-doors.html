Bespoke Timber Patio Doors

Patio doors connect your home to the garden, terrace, or balcony. Whether you prefer the wide views of a sliding patio door or the classic elegance of French patio doors, we manufacture both styles from engineered timber — built to your exact opening dimensions.

Timber patio doors outperform aluminium and uPVC in thermal efficiency, repairability, and character. Engineered Accoya provides dimensional stability with a 50-year rot guarantee, while our factory spray finish ensures a flawless appearance from day one.

Patio Door Styles

  • Sliding patio doors — 2, 3, or 4 panels glide on precision aluminium tracks. Slim sightlines, maximum glass area. Panels stack behind each other — no swing space needed. Ideal for wide openings where unobstructed views are the priority. See full sliding door specification →
  • French patio doors — two leaves swing open (inward or outward) for a classic, elegant opening. Sequential opening on French pairs. Glazing bar options for period properties. See full French door specification →
  • Bifold patio doors — 2 to 8 panels fold to one side, creating a full-width opening. Ideal for merging indoor and outdoor living. See full bifold door specification →

Why Timber Patio Doors?

Aluminium patio doors dominate the mass market, but timber offers significant advantages. Timber is a natural insulator — it conducts heat 1,500 times less efficiently than aluminium, eliminating cold bridging and reducing energy bills. Timber can be repaired, sanded, and refinished indefinitely; aluminium and uPVC cannot. And timber patio doors suit period properties and Conservation Areas where metal or plastic may be refused by planning officers.

Our engineered construction uses laminated timber sections that resist warping and bowing — the traditional weakness of solid timber doors. Combined with precision manufacturing and factory-applied Sikkens coatings, our patio doors are built to perform for decades.

Specification Highlights

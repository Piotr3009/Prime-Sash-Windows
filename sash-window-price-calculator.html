How the Calculator Works

  1. Enter your dimensions. Add the width and height of each sash window. Not sure how to measure? Our measurement guide walks you through brick-to-brick and exact sizing.
  2. Choose your specification. Pick your glazing (double, triple or passive), colour, Georgian bar pattern and opening style. The price updates live as you go.

What You Can Price

The calculator covers every option that affects the cost of a bespoke sash window:

  • Size — priced per m², with degressive pricing so larger windows cost less per m².
  • Glazing — double (U-value 1.4), triple (1.2) or passive glass (0.8) for ultra-low-energy projects.
  • Colour — white as standard, or any RAL / Farrow & Ball colour, single or dual.
  • Georgian bars — add any pattern and see the exact cost per bar update instantly.
  • Glazing bars, horns & detailing — match the authentic look of your period property.

What Affects Your Price

Size is the biggest factor, followed by glazing type, colour and the number of Georgian bars. A standard bedroom sash (800 × 1200mm) costs roughly half of a large reception window. Non-white colours add 5%, dual colour adds 15%, and each Georgian bar adds £15. Every price includes sealed double-glazed units with warm-edge spacers, PAS24 security and a 10-year warranty. For the full story on what drives the cost, read our complete cost guide.

Sash Window Calculator — FAQ

Is the calculator free to use?

Yes. It's completely free, needs no account, and there's no obligation. Price as many windows as you like, as often as you like.

Is the calculator price my final price?

It's an accurate guide based on what you enter. We confirm the final figure after a quick check — and a site survey if we're installing.

Can I price a whole house?

Yes. Add every window and the calculator totals them for you, applying degressive pricing automatically as the order grows.

An Instant Quote, Built for Timber

Timber windows aren't a catalogue product — the species, glazing and detailing all change the price. That's why a generic quote rarely fits. Our calculator is built specifically for bespoke timber, so the instant figure you see reflects exactly the hardwood window you want, not a rough average.

Why Timber Quotes Differ From uPVC

A uPVC quote is largely fixed: limited sizes, limited colours, one material. Timber is made to measure, so your quote depends on choices uPVC simply can't offer — the species of wood, a stained or painted finish in any shade, authentic glazing bars, and joinery detailing to match a period home. Our instant quote prices all of these live, so you see the real cost of your specification rather than a placeholder.

Choose Your Timber in the Quote

  • Engineered redwood — the most cost-effective species, stable and ideal for a painted finish.
  • Accoya — outstanding stability and a 50-year above-ground guarantee, at a premium over redwood.
  • Oak — the premium hardwood for character and a natural stained finish.

Switch species in the quote and the price updates instantly, so you can weigh upfront cost against decades of performance.

How Your Instant Timber Quote Works

  1. Enter your sizes. Width and height for each timber window.
  2. Choose timber, glazing and finish. The guide price recalculates the moment you change a setting.
  3. Send for a firm quote. One click and we confirm your figure — with a survey before anything is made.

What's Included

Guide prices start from £815 per timber window (supply only, ex-VAT) and include sealed double-glazed units with warm-edge spacers, PAS24 security and a 10-year warranty. There's no obligation and no sales call — price as many timber windows as you like and proceed only when you're ready.

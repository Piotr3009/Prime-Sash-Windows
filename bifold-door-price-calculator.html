Price Your Bifold Doors Online

Bifold doors are priced mainly on how many panels you need and the overall size of the opening. Our calculator handles the panel maths for you — set your configuration and see an itemised guide price straight away, then turn it into a firm quote when you're ready.

How the Calculator Works

  1. Enter your opening width and height. The calculator works out sensible panel sizes for you.
  2. Choose your panels and fold. Pick the number of panels, fold direction, glazing and threshold. The price updates live.
  3. See your guide price. Itemised and obligation-free, ready for a firm quote.

How Many Panels Do You Need?

Bifolds run from two to eight panels. More panels means a wider opening — and a higher price, because each panel adds glass, framing and hardware:

  • 2–3 panels — ideal for a standard patio opening up to around 2.7m wide.
  • 4–5 panels — a generous opening for larger rooms, up to around 4.5m.
  • 6–8 panels — opens an entire wall to the garden for an extension or orangery.

Each panel has a maximum width for smooth folding, so very wide openings simply use more panels. The calculator keeps every panel within spec automatically.

What You Can Price

  • Panel count — 2 to 8, with the fold-side configurable.
  • Fold direction — fold inward or outward, stacking left, right or split.
  • Glazing — double, triple or passive glass across the run.
  • Threshold — standard or low-level for step-free access.

Bifold Door Calculator — FAQ

How many bifold panels do I need?

It depends on your opening width: 2–3 panels suit openings up to about 2.7m, 4–5 panels up to about 4.5m, and 6–8 panels open an entire wall. The calculator suggests sensible panel sizes from your dimensions.

What's the maximum width of a bifold panel?

Each panel has a maximum width for smooth folding and weather sealing. Wider openings simply use more panels — the calculator keeps every panel within spec automatically.

Does the price depend on the number of panels?

Yes — panel count is the main driver. Each panel adds glass, framing and hardware, so a 2-panel set starts from £3,970 and prices rise with each added panel.

Can the doors fold inward or outward?

Yes — you can choose the fold direction and which side the panels stack, to suit your room layout and the way you use the space.

Can I have a low threshold?

Yes — a low-level threshold gives near step-free access between inside and the garden. You can select it in the calculator and it's reflected in the price.

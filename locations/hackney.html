Hackney's Period Window Experts

Hackney has experienced a remarkable renaissance, with its Victorian terraces and Georgian squares becoming some of London's most sought-after properties. From the elegant streets of De Beauvoir Town to the historic properties of Clapton Square, these homes deserve windows that match their architectural significance. At Prime Sash Windows, we specialise in crafting authentic timber sash windows that enhance Hackney's period character.

Hackney contains several important Conservation Areas including De Beauvoir, Clapton Square, Mare Street, and London Fields. Our team has extensive experience working within these protected zones, creating windows that meet heritage requirements while delivering modern thermal and acoustic performance.

Why Hackney Homeowners Choose Us

Our timber sash windows are crafted exclusively from premium hardwood and engineered timber, preventing warping in London's variable climate. We use traditional joinery techniques combined with modern performance features including slimline double glazing, draught-proofing, and PAS24 security certification.

Every window is finished with our premium spray system using Sikkens Wood Coatings and Teknos products, ensuring a flawless finish that lasts for decades. As FENSA registered installers, we handle everything from initial survey to final installation, giving you complete peace of mind.

Fulham's Period Window Experts

Fulham has become one of London's most desirable neighbourhoods, prized for its handsome Victorian terraces, spacious Edwardian family homes, and riverside properties along the Thames. From the elegant streets of Parsons Green to the period properties of Hurlingham, these homes deserve windows that honour their architectural heritage. At Prime Sash Windows, we specialise in crafting authentic timber sash windows that complement Fulham's period character.

Fulham contains several Conservation Areas managed by Hammersmith and Fulham Council, including the Moore Park, Crabtree, and Hurlingham Conservation Areas. Our team has extensive experience navigating the planning requirements for these protected areas, creating windows that satisfy heritage officers while delivering modern thermal and acoustic performance.

Why Fulham Homeowners Choose Us

Our timber sash windows are crafted exclusively from premium hardwood and engineered timber, preventing warping in London's variable climate. We use traditional joinery techniques combined with modern performance features including slimline double glazing, draught-proofing, and PAS24 security certification.

Every window is finished with our premium spray system using Sikkens Wood Coatings and Teknos products, ensuring a flawless finish that lasts for decades. As FENSA registered installers, we handle everything from initial survey to final installation, giving you complete peace of mind.

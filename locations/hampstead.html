Hampstead's Period Window Experts

Hampstead represents the pinnacle of London's period architecture. From the grand Georgian mansions overlooking the Heath to the elegant Victorian villas along Flask Walk, these prestigious properties demand windows of the highest quality. At Prime Sash Windows, we specialise in creating bespoke timber sash windows that meet the exacting standards Hampstead homeowners expect.

Much of Hampstead falls within strictly protected Conservation Areas, with many properties holding Listed Building status. Our craftsmen have extensive experience navigating the stringent planning requirements of Hampstead, Camden Council, and English Heritage, ensuring your windows are both authentically designed and fully compliant.

Why Hampstead Homeowners Choose Us

Our timber sash windows are crafted exclusively from premium hardwood and engineered timber, preventing warping in London's variable climate. We use traditional joinery techniques combined with modern performance features including slimline double glazing, draught-proofing, and PAS24 security certification.

Every window is finished with our premium spray system using Sikkens Wood Coatings and Teknos products, ensuring a flawless finish that lasts for decades. As FENSA registered installers, we handle everything from initial survey to final installation, giving you complete peace of mind.

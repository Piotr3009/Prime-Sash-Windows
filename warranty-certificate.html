What Is Not Covered

This warranty does not extend to:

  1. Normal wear and tear, including gradual fading of colour due to UV exposure.
  2. Damage caused by misuse, abuse, accident, fire, flood, storm, or other acts of God.
  3. Condensation forming on the interior surface of glass — this is a ventilation/humidity issue within the property, not a product defect.
  4. Any modification, alteration, repair, or painting carried out by any party other than Skylon Joinery LTD without prior written consent.
  5. Failure to carry out the required maintenance programme as detailed in this document.
  6. Movement or settlement of the building structure affecting window or door openings.
  7. Products installed at properties outside of the United Kingdom.
  8. Minor cosmetic imperfections in timber that do not affect performance (e.g. natural knots, grain variation, minor colour differences between units).
  9. Damage from unsuitable cleaning agents, abrasive materials, or pressure washers.
  10. Problems resulting from inadequate ventilation within the property.

Prime Sash Windows Maintenance Guide

Your Maintenance Guide

Regular maintenance is essential to preserve the beauty and performance of your timber windows and doors — and to keep your 10-year warranty valid. Please follow this schedule carefully.

Maintenance must be carried out at least every 2 years. Failure to maintain your products will invalidate the warranty. We recommend an annual inspection with a full maintenance service every two years.

🔍
Inspect

Check all timber surfaces for signs of paint deterioration, cracks, or early signs of moisture ingress. Pay special attention to horizontal surfaces (sills, bottom rails) where water can collect.

🎨
Re-coat

Lightly sand any areas where paint has deteriorated and apply a fresh coat of high-quality exterior microporous paint. All exposed timber must remain fully sealed at all times.

⚙
Lubricate

Apply a light machine oil or silicone-based lubricant to all hinges, locks, spiral balances, and moving parts. Wipe away excess. This prevents stiffness and premature wear.

🧹
Clean

Clean glass with a standard glass cleaner. Clean timber frames with warm soapy water and a soft cloth. Never use abrasive cleaners, solvents, or pressure washers on timber or sealed units.

Bi-Annual Maintenance Checklist

  • Inspect all external timber surfaces for paint/stain deterioration
  • Re-coat any areas showing paint breakdown (sand lightly first)
  • Check all sills and bottom rails for standing water or moisture damage
  • Inspect weatherseals and draught strips — replace if compressed or damaged
  • Lubricate all hinges, locks, handles, and spiral balances
  • Check that all windows and doors open, close, and lock smoothly
  • Clean all glass units — check for signs of seal failure (internal misting)
  • Clear drainage channels and trickle vents of debris
  • Inspect mastic/sealant around frames — re-apply if cracked or shrunk
  • Check glazing beads are secure and seated properly

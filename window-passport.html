Built for the golden thread

Digital traceability, before it was required

The UK Building Safety Act calls for a “golden thread” of building information — a digital record of what was installed, by whom, and to what specification. Every Prime Sash Windows product already carries one. For architects, builders and building owners, that is compliance built in, not bolted on: the record lives at the product itself, not in a filing cabinet.

Questions

What is a Window Passport?

A permanent digital record attached to every window and door we manufacture. A small engraved QR plate on the frame links to the window's full specification, live warranty status, technical drawing and documents. No app or login is needed — any phone camera opens it.

Does it transfer when a property is sold?

Yes. The passport belongs to the window, not to a customer account. Whoever scans the plate — a new owner, a surveyor or a tradesperson — sees the same permanent record, including the warranty, which transfers with the property.

How does it support the golden thread?

The Building Safety Act calls for a reliable digital record of what was installed, to what specification. Each of our products carries exactly that — a digitally stored specification and document trail, accessible at the installed product itself.

Is my information visible to anyone who scans it?

The passport shows the window's technical record. It is deliberately excluded from search engines, and the plate must be physically scanned at the window — the record cannot be found by searching an address online.

Bespoke Timber French Doors

French doors are a timeless choice — two leaves that swing open to connect your interior with a garden, balcony, or terrace. They suit every style of property, from Georgian townhouses to modern extensions. At Prime Sash Windows, we manufacture bespoke timber French doors as single doors or paired leaves, opening inward or outward, built to your exact dimensions.

Unlike mass-produced uPVC alternatives, our timber French doors are built from engineered Accoya or hardwood — dimensionally stable, rot-resistant, and guaranteed for decades. The result is a door with authentic proportions, narrow sightlines, and the warm character that only real timber provides.

Specification

  • Configuration — single door or French pair (double). Sequential opening on French pairs.
  • Opening — inward or outward opening. Frame automatically mirrors for inward configuration.
  • Timber — engineered Accoya or hardwood. 50-year rot guarantee (Accoya).
  • Glazing — double glazed 4/16/4 (U-value 1.4) or triple glazed.
  • Security — PAS24 multi-point locking on both leaves. Shoot bolts top and bottom.
  • Weather bar — 40mm × 30mm aluminium weather bar for outward-opening doors.
  • Threshold — Standard hardwood, aluminium low-profile, or flush.
  • Colour — any RAL colour, full Farrow & Ball range. Dual colour available.
  • Glazing bars — Georgian bars, cottage bars, or plain glass.

French Doors for Period Properties

French doors have been a feature of British architecture since the 17th century. In Conservation Areas and Listed buildings, replacements must match the original style — narrow meeting stiles, period-correct moulding profiles, and authentic proportions. Our French doors replicate these details precisely while incorporating modern performance features: multi-point locking, draught seals, and thermally efficient glazing.

For period properties, we can add Georgian glazing bars — either planted (applied to the glass surface) or through-bars (integrated into the frame). Both options are available in our 3D configurator.

How It Works

  • 1. Design online — Use our 3D configurator to design and price your French doors instantly.
  • 2. Free survey — We visit your property, measure the opening, and confirm the specification.
  • 3. Manufacture — Your doors are built in our workshop. Typical lead time 4–5 weeks.
  • 4. Installation — Our FENSA-certified team installs your doors with minimal disruption.

Price Your Timber Doors Online

From a single entrance door to a six-panel bifold, our calculator prices every type of bespoke timber door. Choose your door style, glazing and ironmongery and see an itemised guide price straight away — then turn it into a firm quote when you're ready.

How the Calculator Works

  1. Choose your door type. French, bifold, sliding or single — each has its own configuration.
  2. Set your size and spec. Dimensions, glazing, colour, ironmongery and any side panels. The price updates live.
  3. See your guide price. Itemised and obligation-free, ready to turn into a firm quote.

Door Types You Can Price

Each door type has a dedicated calculator path — pick the one that fits your opening:

  • French doors — a classic double-door pair, inward or outward opening, with optional side panels. Price french doors →
  • Bifold doors — two to eight panels that fold back to open a wall to the garden. Price bifold doors →
  • Sliding & patio doors — large glazed panels that slide rather than swing. See sliding doors →
  • Single entrance doors — a solid or glazed single door for front, back or side entrances.

What Affects Door Pricing

  • Door type & size — bifolds and sliders cost more than a single door because of the engineering and hardware.
  • Glazing — double, triple or passive glass, and how much of the door is glazed.
  • Ironmongery — handles, locks, hinges and tracks in your chosen finish.
  • Side panels & fanlights — extra glazed sections beside or above the door.

Timber Door Calculator — FAQ

Which door type is right for me?

French doors suit a classic double-door opening; bifolds open up a whole wall to the garden; sliding doors maximise glass in a fixed run; single doors suit entrances. Each has its own calculator path.

Can I price doors and windows together?

Yes — add doors and windows to the same project in the calculator and it totals everything, so you can budget a whole renovation in one place.

Is ironmongery included in the price?

The guide price includes standard ironmongery for the door type. You can choose upgraded handles, locks and finishes, and the price updates accordingly.

Can I add side panels or a fanlight?

Yes — fixed glazed panels beside the door (side panels) and above it (fanlights) can be configured and are added to the price.

What's included in the price?

Supply-only prices include sealed double-glazed units, weatherseals, standard ironmongery and a 10-year warranty. Installation is quoted after a survey.

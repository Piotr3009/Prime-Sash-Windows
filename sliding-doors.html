Bespoke Timber Sliding Doors

Sliding doors offer the widest unobstructed views of any door type. The panels glide horizontally on precision tracks — no swing space required, no folding mechanism to maintain. At Prime Sash Windows, we manufacture bespoke timber sliding doors in 2, 3, and 4 panel configurations, built to your exact opening dimensions.

Our timber sliding doors combine slim sightlines with generous glass areas. The result is a door that frames your garden like a picture window, while providing easy access and excellent thermal performance.

Sliding vs Bifold Doors

Bifold doors fold open to create a full-width opening — ideal when you want to merge indoor and outdoor space completely. Sliding doors keep their panels in the frame at all times, giving you cleaner sightlines and a more architectural look. They also require less wall space because panels stack behind each other rather than folding to one side.

For wide openings where maximum glass and minimal frame are the priority, sliding doors are hard to beat. For full open-plan access to a garden, bifolds win.
